- `index.html`: Complete portfolio featuring your bio, **E-Netra** anti-shoulder-surfing project, **CapCut Visual Studio**, **@hari_thenics** creator hub, College Expo & Chairman's Briefing milestones, Java/Cybersecurity skills, Freelance booking banner, and Badminton/Chess challenge card.

- `script.js`: Interactive Red-Team swarm particle engine with cursor tethers, repulsion physics, and a live `MODE: HYBRID_RED / PURE_CRIMSON` switcher in the top navbar.
- `profile.jpg`: Your uploaded portrait photo, pre-linked and ready to display.

## How to Put It Live on GitHub Pages (2 Minutes)
1. **Extract this ZIP file** to a folder on your computer or phone.
2. Log in to your GitHub account (**https://github.com/hariom0003k**) and create a **New Repository** (e.g., `portfolio` or `hariom0003k.github.io`).
3. Click **Add file -> Upload files** and drag all 4 extracted files (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, and `profile.jpg`) into the root of the repository, then click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings -> Pages** inside your repository, set **Branch** to `main` (and `/root`), and click **Save**.
5. Your live website will be online at `https://hariom0003k.github.io/<repo-name>/`!
